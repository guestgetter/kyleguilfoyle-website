# Homepage direction note

## Direction

**FOR:** Restaurant owners, operators, and builders who should feel within two seconds that Kyle has worked inside the problem and sees a sharper system than "do more marketing."

**DIRECTION:** A dense editorial field notebook. Warm, human, and assured. Airy at the thesis, denser as proof accumulates. Contemporary structure with analog margin notes. Polished enough to trust, hand-touched enough to feel lived in.

**STEALING:**

- Maggie Appleton: the oversized editorial thesis, generous first viewport, and a visible taxonomy that makes a body of thinking feel explorable.
- Craig Mod: one real photograph doing the work of a dozen claims, restrained navigation, and print-like pacing.
- PostHog: honest information density and interface-shaped proof. Borrow the confidence to show the system, not the desktop metaphor or novelty navigation.

**SIGNATURE:** The Regulars Loop, a mint marker line that moves from first visit to remembered to regular. It starts as a margin annotation in the hero and becomes the organizing device for the page.

## Baseline diagnosis

- Floating food emoji read as decoration instead of personality.
- Gradient headline text and generic cards dilute the point of view.
- The current slider asks the visitor to interact before it has earned attention.
- Claims about predictable revenue and stress-free operations are presented without proof.
- Mobile is a collapsed desktop composition rather than its own first impression.

## Constraints

- Use only existing, real photography and published writing.
- Do not invent metrics, testimonials, logos, or client outcomes.
- Preserve the existing information architecture and working routes.
- No emoji icons, gradient text, glow effects, glass panels, or three-card feature grids.
- One display face, one text face, and one mono face only where data or annotation needs it.
- Respect reduced motion, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, and 44px tap targets.

## Review target

The page must score at least 7/10 on distinctiveness, hierarchy, craft, desire, honesty, brand fit, and mobile integrity at both 390px and 1440px. If the third review round still misses, reject the direction rather than polishing it indefinitely.

### Final rubric

| Axis | Score | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Distinctiveness | 9 | The Regulars Loop, editorial scale, and marker annotation belong to this point of view. |
| Hierarchy | 9 | Thesis first, operating loop second, lived proof third, then the body of work. |
| Craft | 8 | Consistent type, spacing, alignment, hover, focus, and responsive behavior. |
| Desire | 8 | Makes the operator's problem feel understood without promising an unsupported outcome. |
| Honesty | 9 | Existing photographs, published writing, verified dates, and no invented metrics or proof. |

**Rejected from the baseline:** floating food emoji, gradient headline text, the transformation slider, unsupported outcome cards, and the generic feature-card rhythm.

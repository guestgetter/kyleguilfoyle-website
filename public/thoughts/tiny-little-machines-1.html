I want my business run by AI agents and tuned by me

I help restaurants and other local shops get more customers, mostly with Google and Meta ads. For years that meant living inside those platforms. Now I'd rather work from one place, with AI connected to all of it.

For that I need what I call a cockpit. A dashboard shows you the data. A cockpit pulls it in from Google Ads, Meta Ads and Search Console, helps you read it, and helps you act on it.

This is episode 1 of Tiny Little Machines. I build this stuff all the time and rarely share it, so I'm making it a habit.

If you want to start your own cockpit, take inventory first:

  1. What does your business depend on you remembering?
  2. What results do you deliver for your customers?
  3. How do you measure those results?
  4. Which tools would you love to never log into again?

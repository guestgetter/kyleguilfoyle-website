3 Resources Helping Me Learn AI

I love learning about AI - how to build with it, power things with it, and make cool machines with it.

But when I first got interested, I had a hard time figuring out where to start. I wanted to learn how to actually make things, while also understanding where all of this might go.

These three resources have helped me:

  • Matt Pocock’s free AI Skills for Real Engineers course. He takes things that sound boring - like engineering fundamentals - and makes them insanely useful and fun. It’s clear he has a teacher’s heart.
  • Nate B. Jones on YouTube. Very level-headed, no hype.
  • Moonshots with Peter Diamandis. A bunch of super nerds exploring the frontier and where this all might go. The episodes can be long, so I pick whichever one has a title that really grabs me.

That’s been a good mix for me: learning how to build, keeping up with what’s happening, and staying curious about what comes next.

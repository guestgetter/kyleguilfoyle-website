A Curriculum I’d Want to DEVOUR

I could barely get through my university readings.

Now I’m 38 and building a curriculum I’d want to DEVOUR.

Back in 2006, I was 17 and had no idea what to study.

I ended up in a general bachelor program.

After a few months I was like… this is not for me.

So I went into music. At least that was something I could apply and use right away.

I think I really liked the fast feedback loop.

But there were still readings - and I'm an INSANELY slow reader.

They were either too dense or too long and my brain would just kinda… short-circuit.

(I honestly don’t know how I graduated lol…)

So I’m working with AI to build my own curriculum.

One that's FUN and effective for the way my brain works.

Fast feedback.

If I were 17 today...

I would cozy up with a super smart LLM like Astra or Fable and feed it:

  • My dreams
  • My goals in the next 5, 10, 20 years
  • Things I’m curious about
  • People I admire and why
  • What my ideal day looks like
  • Who I want to become
  • Skills I'd love to have

Then ask:

Can you interview me to go deeper so that we can build out the perfect curriculum that isn’t boring and will help me become the kind of person who achieves these goals?

Actually I’d do this at any age.

Here’s what mine’s looking like right now.

Now to come up with a badass name for my program.

Hmu if you have any ideas!
